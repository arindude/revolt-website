Open Role

Business Development Manager (Commission-Only)

Location: Greater Boston / Massachusetts

Core Responsibilities

Site & Partnership Development
  • Identify high-traffic, high-impact locations for EV charging deployment (multifamily, commercial, retail, hospitality, etc.).
  • Utilize GIS tools, EV adoption trends, and utility funding programs to prioritize site outreach.
  • Conduct outbound lead generation and build a prospect pipeline via networking, cold outreach, inbound inquiries, and partnerships.
Relationship Building & Sales Execution
  • Cultivate and maintain relationships with property owners, real estate developers, municipalities, and fleet operators.
  • Pitch Re-Volt’s EV charging solutions and partnership models through presentations and site host engagements.
  • Negotiate long-term agreements with site hosts for EV charging station deployment, including service terms and incentive-aligned partnership models.
  • Integrate available utility rebates and local/state incentive programs into proposals to strengthen deal economics.
  • Support public-sector engagement as needed in coordination with internal stakeholders, prioritizing local opportunities over federal programs like NEVI.
CRM & Reporting
  • Track all business development activities using CRM tools (HubSpot).
  • Provide regular sales reporting, monitor KPIs, and optimize deal flow based on market feedback.
  • Collaborate cross-functionally with engineering, operations, and grant teams to ensure proposal accuracy and fulfillment.

Ideal Candidate Profile

  • 3–5+ years of direct experience in the EV charging industry with a strong track record in business development, sales, or real estate partnerships.
  • 3+ years of experience in site acquisition, energy rebate programs, or municipal permitting.
  • Clear communicator with strong presentation and negotiation skills.
  • Organized, driven, and comfortable working independently in a commission-only role.
  • Proficient in HubSpot for sales tracking/reporting and advanced Excel for financial modeling.
  • Based in Massachusetts and familiar with the New England EV landscape, including local regulations and permitting processes.
  • Willing to attend industry events, site visits, and stakeholder meetings (occasional travel required).

Compensation

  • Commission-only with high earning potential.
  • Performance will be evaluated after 3–6 months; base salary may be considered based on milestones and pipeline development.

Equity Potential

Top-performing candidates who demonstrate a strong commitment to Re-Volt’s long-term success will be eligible for equity consideration.

Additional Benefits

  • Purpose-Driven Mission: Join a team passionate about expanding access to clean energy and delivering impact in underserved communities.
  • Professional Development: Gain valuable experience in the rapidly growing EV charging industry.
