Why invest in EV charging?

“As more people switch to electric vehicles (EVs), providing charging stations is becoming an essential amenity. Offering EV charging can increase property value, attract more visitors, and support sustainability efforts — all while taking advantage of financial incentives to help cover costs.”

Benefits for commercial owners

  • Save money with incentives: MassEVIP and utility rebates offset installation for hospitality and retail sites.
  • Partner with utility providers: Integrate make-ready funding to upgrade infrastructure efficiently.
  • Support sustainability goals: Meet ESG commitments and differentiate your brand.

Drive revenue & efficiency

  • Smart energy use: Advanced load management balances demand across peak periods.
  • Future-proof operations: Keep your destination competitive as EV adoption accelerates.

Elevate guest & shopper experience

  • Valuable amenity: Charging extends dwell time for shoppers and hotel guests.
  • Increase satisfaction: Convenience drives loyalty and repeat visits.
  • Boost partnerships: Attract co-marketing opportunities with EV-forward brands.
