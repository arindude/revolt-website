Anel Bellevue

Co-founder & CEO

Anel Bellevue is a technology entrepreneur and strategic leader focused on expanding equitable access to EV charging. At Re-Volt, he oversees vision, partnerships, and the investments that help property owners electrify faster. Anel brings a track record of building and scaling mission-driven companies while keeping communities at the center of the work.

He collaborates closely with public agencies, utilities, and real estate operators to ensure Re-Volt programs deliver dependable uptime, measurable sustainability outcomes, and great driver experiences.
