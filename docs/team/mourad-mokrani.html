Mourad Mokrani

Co-founder & Chief Business Officer

Mourad Mokrani leads day-to-day operations, partnerships, and go-to-market strategy for Re-Volt Charging. Currently a finance student at Bentley University, he blends financial rigor with on-the-ground experience helping municipalities and enterprises adopt clean technologies.

Prior to Re-Volt, Mourad held roles at the Town of Lexington, Third Rock Ventures, and Thermo Fisher Scientific, and founded VictUHealth to advance wellness access. He brings this operational depth to every charging deployment, ensuring projects pencil out while creating long-term value for stakeholders.
