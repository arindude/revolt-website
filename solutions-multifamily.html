Why invest in EV charging?

“As more people switch to electric vehicles (EVs), providing charging stations at your multi-family property is becoming an essential amenity. Offering EV charging can increase property value, attract more tenants, and support sustainability efforts — all while taking advantage of financial incentives to help cover costs.”

Benefits for property owners

  • Save money with incentives: MassEVIP and utility rebates can reduce installation costs.
  • Partner with utility providers: Energy companies offer programs to lower costs and improve efficiency.
  • Support sustainability goals: Meet environmental commitments and comply with new regulations.

Increase revenue & improve efficiency

  • Smart energy use: Advanced technology balances power distribution, reduces costs, and prevents electrical issues.
  • Future-proof your property: As EV adoption grows, chargers keep properties competitive and desirable.

Attract & retain tenants

  • Valuable amenity: EV charging makes properties more appealing to tenants.
  • Increase tenant satisfaction: Convenience encourages lease renewals.
  • Enhance business appeal: Retail and workplaces benefit from higher foot traffic and employee satisfaction.
